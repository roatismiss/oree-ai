## About Section

Twenty-seven years inside complex organizations taught me to recognize large-scale change in any form it takes.

At Fonds de solidarité FTQ, I lived AI adoption from the inside — its risks, its resistance, its blind spots. At Bell, Bombardier, CGI, Banque Nationale, and Université de Montréal, it was other major transformations, each different from the last, that shaped me.

Today I run Orée Conseil, an independent AI governance consultancy for regulated professionals in Québec: notaries, law firms, certified human resources professionals, and investment firms.

The practice exists because the professions with the most to gain from AI are the ones least able to adopt it carelessly. A notary cannot experiment with a client file. A law firm cannot explain away a draft that went out wrong.

So the work is built the other way around. Obligations first, tools second. Every recommendation has to survive a review by the people entitled to conduct one, and every automation ends where a human signature begins.

**The method: O·R·É·E**
Observe · Uncover · Evaluate · Engage
Three days inside your practice, one costed plan you approve line by line.

**The refrain:**
L'IA rédige. L'humain décide.
AI drafts. The human decides.

Fully independent. No software to sell, no vendor commission to collect.
