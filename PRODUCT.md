# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two first-class audiences (client brief, July 30 2026): (1) leaders of Quebec SMEs across all sectors who are investing in AI without knowing where it creates value, and (2) skeptical partners and principals in regulated Quebec professions: notaries (Vision 2030, Fall 2026 pilot), lawyers (Barreau obligations), CHRPs/HR advisors, and investment firms. They search Google for "AI for lawyers / notaries / accountants", fear client-data leaks and professional-liability risk, and distrust AI hype. French-first, bilingual FR/EN market.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Oree Conseil, the independent AI consulting practice of Aminata Diabate (27 years inside Quebec's most complex organizations; contact aminata.diabate@oreeconseil.ca, Montreal - Eastern Townships). The site must convert skepticism into booked Oree Diagnostic calls (3 days, 6-8 interviews, optional workshop, executive debrief, delivered within two weeks, fixed fee quoted on request) by letting visitors test live AI demos in the browser: proof, not promise. Offer set is three: Diagnostic, Training, Support. Career employers (FTQ, Bell, Bombardier, CGI, Banque Nationale, UdeM) are background, never presented as clients. Success = discovery calls booked before the fall notarial pilot.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credentialed, independent. An advisor's voice, not a vendor's. The refrain « L'IA redige. L'humain decide. » anchors everything: AI drafts, the human signs. Editorial warmth (charcoal/cream/olive/amber, serif) over tech-startup gloss.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient text, glassmorphism, hero metrics, purple-on-dark AI cliches.
- The OrbAI-style neumorphic template is a structural reference only (section rhythm, card visuals, pill marquee); never copy its grayscale palette, sans-serif voice, or English copy 1:1.
- Anything that reads "startup" rather than "cabinet-conseil": no buzzwords, no fake urgency, no stock photos of robots.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what you preach: every AI element on the site shows a human checkpoint, mirroring the method being sold.
2. Proof before promise: demos, method steps, and concrete numbers (3 days, Law 25, data in Canada) beat adjectives.
3. One profession at a time: copy speaks the vocabulary, obligations and fears of a specific profession, never "businesses" in general.
4. French first: per the Site Launch Plan (10 August 2026), FR (Quebec) is the default locale and sits at the root URLs; EN mirrors it under /en behind the nav toggle. This supersedes the original English-first brief, because the audience is Quebec. French copy follows OQLF conventions (guillemets, espace insecable before deux-points only), and wherever oree-conseil.ca already carried Aminata's own French, that wording is reused verbatim rather than retranslated.
5. Editorial calm: the Oree identity (serif, cream, olive, amber, mono overlines) is already committed; preserve it rather than re-inventing per page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text >= 4.5:1 contrast on cream; amber/olive used for text only in darkened variants. prefers-reduced-motion alternatives for every animation (ripples, marquee, floats). Keyboard-navigable menus, focus-visible states, semantic landmarks. No emojis anywhere in UI or content.
